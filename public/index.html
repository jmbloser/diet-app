About meal analysis

Meal photos are sent to Claude (Anthropic's AI) for identification and nutrition estimation. If Claude can't confidently identify everything on your plate, it will ask a clarifying question before you confirm the log. Estimates are approximate — always a good idea to sanity-check against packaging or a nutrition database for precise tracking.
